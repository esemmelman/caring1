Privacy policy

Caring Nearby is a private, invitation-only community directory. It stores member names, contact details, addresses, and approximate coordinates in Supabase so authorized users can find nearby members.

Authentication and hosting

Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and server-side functions. GitHub Pages serves the website. These providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services.

Google Maps Platform

Member street addresses are sent server-side to Google Maps Platform for geocoding and route calculations. Names, email addresses, and phone numbers are not included in those requests. Coordinates obtained through Google are refreshed on a rolling schedule and are not retained for more than 29 days without refresh.

Google’s processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms.

Access and use

Directory data is available to people with the shared access passcode. Designated administrators may add, edit, or delete directory records. The data is not intended for advertising, resale, or public distribution.
